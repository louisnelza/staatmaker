Not tax advice. Staatmaker organises your own records, so check every total against your certificates before you file.

What this is: a private, in-browser tool that reads your own bank statements (CSV or PDF), sorts spending into categories, and totals up items commonly relevant to a South African individual tax return (retirement annuity, medical aid, donations, income protection).

What it isn't: tax advice or a SARS-ready submission. Always check totals against your actual certificates (IRP5, medical aid tax certificate, RA contribution certificate, S18A receipts) before you file.

Where your data lives: files are parsed on your device and saved only in this browser's own storage. That storage is tied to this browser and can be cleared by clearing site data, so download a backup from the Data panel regularly.

How this works

Tag rental deposits as Rental Income and property costs using the Rental – … categories in the Transactions tab. When only part of a property is let, costs that serve the whole property (rates, insurance, electricity, water, bond interest and so on) are cut down to the cottage share, and costs that belong to the cottage alone are claimed in full. Every category has a default treatment below, and you can change any of them.

Cottage share

Rent owed but not yet paid

Rent is taxed when it is received or when you become entitled to it, whichever comes first. Your bank records only show what was received, so enter rent you were owed at each year end (for example, a lease fee for the year that was only partly paid). Rent owed at the start of the year was already taxed last year, so it is taken off when it is paid.

Optional: for cap comparisons

The retirement annuity and donation deductions are capped as a percentage of taxable income. Add an estimate to see how your projected spending compares, or leave it blank to just see projected totals.

Projections simply scale up what you've spent so far this tax year at the same daily rate. They don't know about bonuses, once-off donations, or spending that isn't evenly spread across the year, so treat them as a rough steer rather than a forecast. RA and donation caps shown use the standard SARS percentages; your actual limit also depends on how "taxable income" or "remuneration" is defined in your case, which a tax practitioner can confirm.
